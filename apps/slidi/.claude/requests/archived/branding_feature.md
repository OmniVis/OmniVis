# Feature Request: Branding Feature

## Description
A comprehensive branding system that allows users to customize presentations with their company identity. This includes logos, names, and specific color themes.

## Requirements
- **Company Profile:** Upload company logo and store company name.
- **Color Themes:** Support for custom color palettes associated with the brand.
- **Application:** Automatically apply branding to slides (e.g., logo in the top right corner with company name next to it).
- **Brand Management:** Capability to save and manage multiple brandings.
- **Community Presets:** Allow users to share their branding presets and choose from others created by the community.
- [x] Full branding system implemented with logo/color support and D1-backed presets.
