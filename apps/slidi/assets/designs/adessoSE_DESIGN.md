## Brand & Style

The design system is built on a foundation of **Modern Corporate** aesthetics mixed with **Agile Dynamism**. It reflects a brand that is technically excellent yet human-centric and approachable. The visual narrative is driven by clarity, professional reliability, and a forward-moving energy (symbolized by the "Vertical" graphic element).

The target audience consists of B2B decision-makers and tech-savvy experts who value both high-level IT competence and authentic, down-to-earth partnership. The UI should evoke a sense of innovation that is grounded in reality—never cold or purely abstract, but always precise and "ready for the now."

**Key Visual Pillars:**
- **The Vertical:** A signature angled line element derived from circuit board patterns, representing the intersection of technology and human service.
- **Nahbarkeit (Approachability):** Authentic imagery and clear, jargon-free layouts.
- **Precision:** Mathematical alignment and consistent use of a geometric, condensed typographic system.

## Colors

The palette is dominated by the signature "adesso-blue," a vibrant, trustworthy primary shade. It is balanced by a sophisticated "adesso-grey" for secondary information and structural elements.

**Gradients:**
This design system utilizes a modular gradient system. Gradients should always originate or end with the primary blue. Use fluid, multi-stop transitions (e.g., Blue to Cyan/Teal or Blue to Magenta) to add depth and "digital energy" to backgrounds and large-scale components. Gradients should feel luminous but never muddy.

**Color Roles:**
- **Primary:** Action states, brand headers, and key accents.
- **Secondary:** Supporting text, borders, and "The Vertical" element in neutral contexts.
- **Tertiary (Accents):** Used sparingly for high-energy highlights (e.g., Teal/Green).
- **Surface:** Use the neutral light grey (#f2f2f2) for background containers to distinguish from pure white (#ffffff) page backgrounds.

## Typography

The system utilizes **Chivo** as a modern, high-performance alternative to Klavika, maintaining the distinctive condensed, geometric character of the brand.

**Usage Guidelines:**
- **Weight Contrast:** Headlines should lean into Bold or Black weights to command attention. Body text remains light to medium for optimal readability.
- **Alignment:** Primarily left-aligned to mirror the "Vertical" structural line. 
- **The "Light" Style:** For internal sub-brands or secondary descriptors, use lighter weights with increased tracking to maintain an airy, technical feel.
- **Hierarchy:** Ensure a clear distinction between the "Display" level (used for campaigns) and "Headline" levels (used for UI architecture).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a **Fixed-Fluid Hybrid Grid**. Content is housed within a 12-column grid system with generous outer margins to emphasize the "clean" and "organized" nature of the brand.

**Structural Principles:**
- **Vertical Alignment:** Elements should often align to a strong left-hand axis, occasionally broken by "The Vertical" graphic element.
- **The Vertical Cut:** Large sections or cards may feature a 45-degree angled corner (the "Vertical") on the bottom-right or top-right to reinforce brand identity.
- **White Space:** Use white space as a functional tool to group related IT services and prevent visual clutter. 
- **Breakpoints:**
  - **Mobile:** 4 columns, 16px margins.
  - **Tablet:** 8 columns, 32px margins.
  - **Desktop:** 12 columns, 64px+ margins (centered container).

## Elevation & Depth

This design system prioritizes **Tonal Layers** and **Low-Contrast Outlines** over heavy shadows. 

**Layering Logic:**
- **Level 0 (Base):** White (#ffffff) or Neutral Light Grey (#f2f2f2).
- **Level 1 (Cards/Containers):** Flat surfaces with a subtle 1px border in #e0e0e0 or using the "Vertical" accent line.
- **Level 2 (Active/Floating):** Used only for dropdowns and modals. Utilize a very soft, diffused shadow (0px 8px 24px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05)) to suggest elevation without breaking the "flat-tech" aesthetic.
- **Interaction:** Depth is primarily communicated through color shifts (e.g., Blue to Darker Blue) rather than physical lifting.

## Shapes

The shape language is **Soft yet Structured**. While the brand is technical, it avoids "sharp" or "aggressive" corners in its UI components to maintain approachability.

**Corner Logic:**
- **Standard UI (Buttons, Inputs):** Subtle 4px (0.25rem) rounding.
- **Special Elements:** The "Vertical" angle is a strict 45-degree cut, never rounded.
- **Pill Shapes:** Used exclusively for tags and "Status" indicators to provide a visual counterpoint to the predominantly rectangular grid.

## Components

**Buttons:**
- **Primary:** Solid #006ab3 background, white text, 4px rounded corners.
- **Secondary:** Ghost style with 1px #006ab3 border and blue text.
- **Icon-Button:** Always paired with a simplified, 2px stroke-width outline icon.

**The "Vertical" Graphic:**
- A recurring UI element. It can be used as a divider, a decorative corner on a card, or a bracket next to the brand logo. It always features a 45-degree angle at its terminus.

**Input Fields:**
- Minimalist design. 1px grey bottom border or full enclosure. Labels use `label-md` and sit above the field.

**Cards:**
- White background, no shadow, 1px light grey border. Often features the "Vertical" as a blue accent line on the left side or a 45-degree cut-out on the bottom right.

**Imagery & Icons:**
- **Icons:** Thin-stroke (2px), modern, and minimalist. No filled shapes unless representing an active/toggled state.
- **Imagery:** Focus on "Nahbarkeit"—real people in bright, clean environments. Avoid overly processed stock photos. Use the primary blue as a subtle overlay or background color in portraits to unify different sources.
